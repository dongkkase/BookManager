Import from your device or computer

  • Copy books and folders from a Mac to an iPhone or iPad with Finder USB file sharing

Connect to NAS, servers and cloud storage

  • Browse shared folders over SMB, FTP, SFTP and WebDAV
  • Browse and import books from OPDS catalogs such as Kavita and Komga
  • Connect to book files in Dropbox and OneDrive
  • Save comics from YACReader Library as CBZ files on your device

Reading comics, webtoons and ebooks

Choose a reading view for your book's format and screen size. Adjust the experience for one-handed reading on a phone or two-page spreads on a tablet.

Automatic view for comics and webtoons

Automatic view scrolls through vertical webtoons and fits other comics to your screen in single pages or spreads. Switch between the original page and a filter preview to compare them.

Text and margins for EPUB and TXT

Mark passages to return to

Highlight a passage in an EPUB or TXT file. In Highlights, filter by book, color or date and open a passage to return to its page.

Reading calendar and device sync

Continue on your phone or tablet

Enable sync to share library information, reading positions, bookmarks and reading history across devices using the same account and platform.

iPhone and iPad use iCloud to sync reading data. Android devices use Google Drive. Sync between iOS and Android is not supported, and the original book files must be imported separately on each device.

Free features and Readive Premium

Importing books and reading or listening on your device is free. Premium adds more fonts and voices, along with detailed reading statistics.

Included for free

  • File imports, library management and remote storage connections
  • Comics, ebooks, audiobooks and text-to-speech with built-in device voices
  • Light, Dark and E-Ink themes and standard fonts
  • Bookmarks, reading positions, basic reading statistics and reading data sync

Readive Premium

  • Remove in-app ads
  • Additional themes, including Dancheong Light and Dark, plus more fonts and custom font imports
  • Supertonic offline voice models
  • Detailed library views and more thumbnails on screen
  • All-time, monthly and yearly reading insights

Premium is available as a monthly or annual subscription or a lifetime purchase. Check the Premium screen in the app for available options and pricing.

Before you get started

How can I start without a book file?

Open Find on the web (Experimental) in the library to browse and download ebooks from Project Gutenberg and audiobooks from LibriVox. Check the source for each title’s terms of use.

Do I need to sign up or use BookManager?

You can import files from your device without creating a Readive account. NAS and cloud connections may require an account with the storage provider. BookManager integration is optional and lets you access your desktop library.

Can I read and listen offline?

Books and audiobooks imported or downloaded to your device are available offline. Browsing and downloading from remote storage, and syncing reading data, require a network connection. With Premium, you can generate Supertonic speech on your device after downloading a voice model.

Are book files automatically copied to my other devices?

Cloud sync shares library information and reading data. Import the original book files separately on each device. Sync works between iPhone and iPad devices using the same account, or between Android devices using the same account.
